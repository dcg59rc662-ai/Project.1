Your files stay here

Photos, platform icons and background clips are read by your browser and used on this page only. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and nothing is shared when you send someone the link.

About platform logos

The badges ship as lettermarks rather than official logos, because those are trademarks with their own usage rules. Download the real icons from each platform's brand resources page and upload them if you want them exact.

Transparency and codecs

Transparent overlays export as WebM with an alpha channel, which Chrome and Edge produce reliably. If your editor shows a black box behind the plate, export the green screen version and key it out instead.

Reading on moving footage

Footage moves and changes brightness, so a plate that looks subtle on a still frame can vanish in motion. A card at eighty percent or higher, or a bold outline, keeps your name legible.

How long should the plate stay on screen?

Four to seven seconds covers most introductions: long enough to read a name and one handle, short enough that it does not overstay. Use the length slider to match the beat of your edit.

Can I use the same design across every video?

That is the point of the library. Save a design, then load it whenever you start a new edit so your plates stay consistent. Export the file to carry your designs to another device.

Why does my export take as long as the animation?

The video is recorded live from the canvas, one frame at a time, so it runs in real time. Keep the tab open and in front while it records.

What does the AI art director actually make?

It plans a concept, then draws original vector pieces such as badges, sparks or frames, places them around your plate and animates each one. Every drawing is checked before it is used, so only plain shapes are allowed through.
